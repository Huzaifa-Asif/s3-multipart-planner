## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-03

### Added

- Deterministic multipart planning with valid S3 part sizes and byte ranges.
- Automatic part-size adjustment for the 10,000-part limit.
- Concurrency-wave and peak-payload-buffer estimates.
- ESM, CommonJS, and TypeScript declarations with zero runtime dependencies.
