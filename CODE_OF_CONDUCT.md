# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

Examples of positive behavior include demonstrating empathy, respecting
different viewpoints, giving and accepting constructive feedback, and taking
responsibility for mistakes. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment,
insults, trolling, publishing others' private information, or conduct that
would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior through the repository owner's public GitHub
profile contact options. Maintainers will respect the reporter's privacy and
may remove, edit, or reject contributions or ban contributors when appropriate.
